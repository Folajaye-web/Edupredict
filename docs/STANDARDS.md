# Standards alignment

EduPredict is a school decision-support application, not a replacement for WAEC/NECO/NABTEB or government assessment.

It aligns its architecture to: curriculum-configurable school levels; continuous assessment components; 0–100 internal scores; Nigerian A1–F9-style reporting bands; longitudinal learner records; attendance; intervention tracking; explainable early warning; and auditable data ingestion.

Official references: NERDC revised BEC/SSEC pages and Federal Ministry quality-assurance materials.
